## Documented facts

- SPP describes `1_` as resource or virtual offers and `0_` as demand or virtual bids. These prefixes do not identify physical versus virtual transactions. Multiple virtual submissions at the same masked settlement location can explain repeated codes in an hour. [SPP historical offers](https://portal.spp.org/groups/im-historical-offers)
- Demand bids are associated with physical load; virtual bids are not. Virtual offers are not associated with a physical resource. [SPP bid glossary](https://www.spp.org/glossary/?term=Bid), [SPP offer glossary](https://www.spp.org/glossary/?term=Offer)
- SPP protocol 118.1 (published June 1, 2026; internal date May 13) confirms slope interpolation, larger-MW endpoint block pricing, and constant endpoint prices (section 4.2.2.1(6), page 103). Price-sensitive demand has a zero minimum and submitted maximum (section 4.2.3.1, pages 142-143). The export flag encoding and physical offer limits remain provisional. [Protocol 118.1 archive](https://spp.org/Documents/76745/Integrated%20Marketplace%20Protocols%20118.1%20-%20Active%20Version.zip)
- An SPP storage filing describes a single offer curve with negative and positive MW for charging and discharging. This establishes a possible interpretation, not the identity of any masked resource in our files. [SPP-hosted FERC order, 2019](https://www.spp.org/Documents/60834/20191017_Order%20on%20Compliance%20-%20Order%20No.%20841%20Compliance%20Filing_ER19-460-000.pdf)
- The supplied CSVs lack explicit commitment status, startup/no-load costs, physical dispatch limits, ramp limits, initial conditions, network constraints, and reserve requirements. See [the local data audit](DATA_AUDIT_20260701.md). We have not established a public source supplying those inputs with a reliable join to these masked rows.

## Network extension assumptions (implemented test-network study)

- **N01 - DC network approximation:** implement connected-network active-power flows from bus voltage angles and branch reactances. Omit reactive power, voltage magnitudes, AC stability and contingency security unless supplied in a later model. MATPOWER DC OPF documents the approximation. Status: authorized test-network extension, not SPP operational topology.
- **N02 - synthetic geography:** use MATPOWER case9 branch topology and fixed-load bus proportions, scaled once using the day's maximum SPP fixed-demand MW. Assign each unprefixed masked code deterministically to one of nine buses by SHA256 with two preselected seeds (0 and 1); repeated codes share a synthetic node, but remain separate submissions. This is neither a physical classification nor a recovered BAA/node mapping. Compare seed sensitivities; do not choose by price fit.
- **N03 - supplied linear loss sensitivities:** optional loss model is total losses = offset + sum(MLSF_i * net_injection_i), with reference-node sensitivity zero. For demonstration, use fixed a priori test coefficients, not historical MLC-derived factors or resistance-derived losses. Loss withdrawal is allocated at the reference bus when reconstructing DC nodal balance/flows; this is an aggregate approximation, not branch-level loss allocation. Require nonnegative accepted total losses; extract MLC=-MEC*MLSF. No claim of actual line losses or full AC feasibility. Coefficients and offsets must be exported with each study.
- **N04 - network stress scenario:** scale case9 baseMVA and branch ratings once by max SPP fixed demand / 315 MW; constrained tests use one preselected rating multiplier, while unconstrained control relaxes all line limits. These ratings are hypothetical for SPP curves, not observed flowgate constraints. No calibration to historical prices; infeasible studies must be reported, never patched with unrecorded capacity increases.
- **N05 - operating limits are explicit inputs only:** solver supports supplied per-row min/max or zero availability inside submitted study bounds. Do not infer physical units, operating limits, ramp rates, commitment states or reserve capability from duplicate codes, case9 generators, OR prices or cleared aggregate outcomes. Those missing SPP inputs remain pending. Test-network constraints demonstrate model mechanics and do not establish greater historical fidelity.

## Quantum candidate experiment assumptions (registered before implementation)

- **Q01 - bounded exact oracle:** Enumerate at most eight explicitly selected commitment bits while every other horizon/unit status stays fixed. Price each full schedule with the original fixed-commitment LP and retain its certificate; assign an infeasible penalty above the largest feasible objective only to schedules with an explicit HiGHS infeasibility status or a documented fixed-status conflict. This finite exact table and its classical preprocessing cost limit scaling and prevent quantum speedup claims. Status: declared research design, not an SPP physical inference. Resolution: replace only with a separately verified scalable encoding and account for its construction cost.
- **Q02 - QAOA choices:** Use a normalized exact diagonal table, independent gamma/beta angles for each layer, depth two by default, COBYLA statevector tuning with seed 101, three restarts and at most 80 iterations each. Initialization and multistart draws are deterministic for a given seed. These are experimental hyperparameters rather than optimum guarantees. Status: declared research design. Resolution: report sensitivity under equal budgets before changing defaults.
- **Q03 - simulator interpretation:** Local statevector tuning and BasicSimulator measurements demonstrate circuit/interface correctness and bounded candidate quality only. They are not IonQ remote execution, operational SPP savings, or a hardware advantage. Include oracle preparation, tuning and sampling costs in comparisons. Status: declared research scope. Resolution: separately record actual backend results and comparable full-cost baselines if executed.
- **Q04 - benchmark neighborhood and budgets:** In the declared 24-hour energy-only case, use the certified MILP commitment as the fixed matrix and vary `test_peak` and `test_local` at zero-based intervals 16 and 17, the two consecutive intervals with the highest combined demand (200.000 and 199.466494 MW). Tiny cases vary startup cost only at the declared $10 and $1000 values. A measured method evaluates exactly `shots` candidate occurrences per repeat; enumeration cycles its 16 exact entries to that occurrence budget and separately reports 16 unique evaluations. One-bit local search uses exactly the same occurrence budget, allowing duplicate visits. All comparisons use each case's identical original objective and feasibility table; table construction, local QAOA tuning, circuit execution, selection, and fresh original-model certificate verification are disclosed separately. Status: declared bounded benchmark convention, not a scalable advantage claim. Resolution: revise only with a separately declared comparison budget and fresh evidence.
- **Q05 - IonQ single-circuit export layout:** The Qollab-selected free IonQ simulator rejected a two-circuit list submission before execution with HTTP 400, “Only job type ionq.circuit.v1 is supported.” The official qiskit-ionq serializer maps list/tuple inputs to `ionq.multi-circuit.v1` and one `QuantumCircuit` to `ionq.circuit.v1`. For the portable upload, place the four-bit uniform candidate on qubits/classical bits 0–3 and the independent four-bit QAOA candidate on bits 4–7 of one eight-qubit circuit. Measure all qubits at the end, submit that single circuit in one backend job, require complete valid eight-bit joint counts, and marginalize the low/high halves to score each original four-bit oracle at the same requested shots. Physical noise may correlate their observed results; no independence or remote-success claim follows from the disjoint ideal circuit. The local benchmark remains its separate measured-circuit comparison. Status: evidence-backed remote compatibility convention. Resolution: change only after a verified backend contract and equivalent result validation.

### UI-GRAPH01: conceptual resource dispatch

The resource graph uses actual masked submissions and signed optimized injections. Its hub/edges represent the single-node balance equation, not physical transmission or geographic resource locations. Before solving, the preview ranks resources by the largest absolute submitted endpoint (capacity only). After solving it ranks by absolute awarded net injection. It shows up to six per side plus aggregate remainder totals; selecting another active resource includes it in that subset. Existing QAOA results are synthetic commitment experiments, not runs on these SPP offers.


### Q-SPP01: bounded actual-SPP dispatch encoding

The new spp_dispatch adapter (distinct from the legacy synthetic commitment experiment) uses up to four real submission quantity decisions. One baseline award level is preserved; the other moves toward greater quantity headroom by min(1 MW, 10% of full submitted range, headroom), upward on a tie. Candidate decisions rank by distance of the baseline marginal-price interval to the full-auction price, then source row ID. These binary dispatch levels are study discretizations, not verified physical unit commitments.

### Q-SPP02: frozen dispatch and algebraic recourse

The actual submission with greatest baseline interior quantity slack balances the neighborhood, ties by source row ID. Remaining submissions stay at certified baseline awards. Signed equality determines balancing quantity exactly, including negative residual demand; source bounds and original integrated costs determine candidate feasibility/cost. The constant frozen-resource cost is included. If no meaningful feasible alternative exists, comparison is unavailable. These are original-curve feasibility checks, not a full-auction optimality certificate for every candidate.

### Q-SPP03: conditional price and comparison evidence

The balancing submission's interior derivative gives a conditional marginal energy price. Kinks show an interval; quantity bounds report no unique price. This is conditional on discrete quantities and frozen awards, not a QAOA dual, full discrete-model marginal value, or nodal SPP LMP. All stochastic methods score the same classically enumerated table. Five local seeded trials at 256 candidate occurrences are descriptive; one-bit samples are dependent. Tuning and preparation are additional overhead, and exact enumeration always identifies the small table optimum. No scalable advantage claim.

### Q-SPP04: local execution and provenance cache

Explicit comparison runs reuse the market worker and use installed Qiskit BasicSimulator only. Settings: seed 2026, depth1, one restart, maxiter40, five trials of 256 candidates. Cache keys include dataset hashes, UTC interval, baseline run identity/awards/demand/price, encoding version and settings. Including baseline run identity conservatively prevents cross-run reuse despite numerically equal awards; repeat requests on the same baseline reuse completed results. Cache is in-process; ignored original provenance/oracle/report artifacts stay project-local.
