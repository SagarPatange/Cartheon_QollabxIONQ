MODEL / DATA GUIDE

Each interval is an independent, continuous, single-node auction. Signed offers and responsive purchase bids use their submitted block/sloped curves. Net injection equals SPP Cleared Fixed Demand Bid. Net offer cost is integrated offer cost minus purchase bid value and may be negative.

Model system price is a simplified marginal price, not nodal SPP LMP. Historical SMP is shown only for context and never enters the solve. Masked resource-to-BAA/geographic mappings, transmission, commitment, inter-hour ramps and reserves are unresolved in this view.

Upload matching DA Energy Offers and DA Market Clearing CSVs. Consecutive same-day subsets are supported and labeled with coverage. UTC interval ends are the identity; CPT labels are shown alongside them. Original SPP data remain excluded from MIT. Uploads and results stay inside this project's ignored local folders. Re-select uploaded files after restarting the server.
