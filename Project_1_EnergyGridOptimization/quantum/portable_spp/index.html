HOW TO INTERPRET RESULTS

Optimal hits count sampled candidates matching the exact 16-state minimum. A zero best cost gap means the best sampled candidate reached that minimum. It does not mean the method is faster or lowers the certified cost.

Net offer cost subtracts responsive purchase-bid value, so it can be negative. Conditional prices come from classical pricing of each fixed candidate, not from QAOA. Selecting a resource shows the baseline and live QAOA quantity; a missing feasible QAOA sample shows unavailable.

The full-market model price is a recorded classical result. It excludes actual network congestion and loss components. These binary choices are study dispatch levels, not physical unit commitments.
