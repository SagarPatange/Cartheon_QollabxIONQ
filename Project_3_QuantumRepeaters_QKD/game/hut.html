Welcome, Mario! Qubits are fragile: a stray bump can flip |0⟩ into |1⟩. You can't just copy a qubit to keep a backup, but you can spread one logical qubit over several physical ones.

Here is the simplest code: |0̄⟩ = |000⟩ and |1̄⟩ = |111⟩. Two parity checks compare neighbours without ever reading the logical bit, so they spot a flip without destroying the superposition.
